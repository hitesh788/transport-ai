Logistics and Shipping FAQ
Q: What is the standard delivery time for domestic shipping?
A: Standard ground shipping typically takes 3-5 business days depending on the distance.

Q: How can I track my shipment?
A: You can track your shipment using the 12-digit tracking number on our website or mobile app.

Q: What are the weight limits for parcel delivery?
A: Individual parcels must not exceed 70 lbs (32 kg). For heavier shipments, please use our freight services.

Q: Do you offer international shipping?
A: Yes, we ship to over 150 countries. International express delivery takes 2-4 business days.
