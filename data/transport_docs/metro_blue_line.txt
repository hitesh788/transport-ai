Metro Line Blue - Airport Link
Connects International Airport to Downtown Hub.
Service Hours: 04:00 - 00:00 Daily.
Frequency: 
- Weekdays: 8 minutes peak, 12 minutes off-peak.
- Weekends: 15 minutes all day.
Key Interchanges: Airport Terminal 1, Airport Terminal 2, Sky Bridge, Commerce Center, Downtown Hub.
Baggage Policy: Passengers are allowed two large suitcases and one carry-on. Priority seating available for elderly and disabled passengers.
Fare: $5.00 flat rate for airport travel.
